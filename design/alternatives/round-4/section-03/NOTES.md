## Portable lessons under review

- Give a section one primary explanatory question. Separate the interpretation of information, the decision to act, the action itself, evidence of its outcome and what persists when those questions would otherwise compete.
- For an execution question, attach checks and stop consequences to the flow they govern. Keep the successful path visible.
- For an interface question, place representative data beside the explanation of each consequential field and the limits of its authority.
- For an acceptance question, compare conditions and consequences directly. Distinguish a condition matrix from an exact execution trace when checks recur.
- Preserve relevant limits beside the affected action. Explain the difference between an intermediate success and the user's observed result without repeating the same caveat in several unconnected boxes.
